Featured internship project · May–Jul 2026

FlyRank — Early-Signal Decline-Risk Triage

Content operations · Machine learning · Decision support

0.70 Precision@20 · Logistic Regression

Built an early-warning workflow to rank eligible content by its likelihood of a decline in clicks per measured day. Compared a rule-based baseline with Logistic Regression and Random Forest using early-period performance and content metadata.

  • Data windows: March 1–15, 2026 for features; March 16–31, 2026 for the outcome proxy.
  • Validation: GroupShuffleSplit by client_hash_id; 13,481 training rows and 3,352 test rows across 22 train clients and 6 test clients.
  • Results: Logistic Regression achieved 0.70 Precision@10, 0.70 Precision@20, and 0.78 Precision@50. Random Forest achieved the highest average precision (0.579) and ROC-AUC (0.542).
  • Decision: Logistic Regression was selected for the human-review action playbook.

Limitations: Single-March proxy evaluation, not multi-month forward validation. It does not establish that content refreshes cause recovery. The executed W05 baseline includes a freshness adjustment and is not strictly comparable with the frozen W04 baseline.
